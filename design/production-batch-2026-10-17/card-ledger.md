## Decisions and uncertain readings (autonomous, not owner-confirmed)

- `temp` (20 conditions) is editorial: stripped, never displayed or compiled, raw value kept in `source.raw.condition`.
- Tag Teams follow the new CSV by ID (7 = B, 11 = J). ID 10's search has no "you can", so it is mandatory; 6, 7, 9 and 11 are optional. All four discounts last this turn only (cost_turn).
- Tag Team J (11): a typeless partner has no Type, so it matches nothing. Option considered: matching other typeless units; rejected because "same Type" on Chaos Witch excludes "?" as a Type.
- Chaos Witch (13): while it is still typeless it imposes nothing on Mages; once its Type changes, every Mage on both sides has the Witch's current Type while it stays on the Field. Option considered: the literal reading that a typeless Witch makes every Mage typeless from the moment it lands; rejected as a large, surprising effect that the "except for ?" clause argues against. Flag for owner review.
- Stormcaller (25): all unprotected Basic Units on both sides, mandatory (decisions.md).
- Dante (36): 6 Burn to all units including Dante (so it starts at 28 + 6 ATK); manual 1 Cleanse or 10 Burn to any unit; +1 ATK per Burn on Dante, continuous.
- Accio (30): one optional effect. Sending a Draw unit to Spent is what enables the Retain; Skip does neither. Retain targets a unit (not any card) in your Hand.
- Sweeper (29) / Last Foundation (63) / Abysslord (59): "Spell or Trap" means a card in a Spell/Trap (backrow) zone. Powers are not included. Abysslord's choice can name a face-down card without revealing it, as every backrow card is a Spell or Trap.
- Magentric (69): "Tributed" means used as a Field material in a Fusion or Ritual summon. Exhausting it from Spent for an Arcane summon, tributing it from Hand (Unstable Fusions), or a Debilitated Magentric gives nothing. The Pierce is applied as the summoned unit lands, before its own On Summon effects.
- Symbiote Larva (156): draw, then discard one card of your choice from Hand to Spent (mandatory).
- Geolock (57): the On Summon is one optional choice for both statuses on all of your units, including itself. The manual ability is offered only when an opponent's unprotected unit exists.
- Solomon, the Tyrannical (181): the protected unit is still chosen explicitly (one option) so the cost is visible and cancellable before it is paid.
- Tyde (172): "Destroy a Unit with a Rune" targets either side's units.
- The Illusionist (177): see decisions.md; optional, your Exhaust only, current ordinary AP cost at most 1; Extra Deck cards never qualify; Necrovast's exception unchanged.
- Divine units: the four On Summon Cleanse values drop to 1; the persistent +1 cost on destruction is unchanged.
- Importer: the exact Arcane requirement text (F03-04) had only been hand-edited into the committed cards.json; it is now an explicit importer correction, so regeneration is deterministic. Text overrides for 90 and 183 were dropped because the export now carries the confirmed wording; 45, 47, 48 and 169 are kept.

## F07 and F09

- **F07**: the separate Fusion Confirm (F04-22 `fusion_confirm`) is removed. The Attack/Defense stance choice is the last, committing step for every Fusion method; Ritual and Arcane already ended at the stance. Every earlier step stays cancellable and consumes nothing; `_fusion_plan_valid` still rechecks the plan after the cost and response window, so stale state fizzles with the cost paid once. Tests: `rules_f04_22_fusion_test` (cancel at every step, stance commits then pays, stale material, Tag Team - S trigger). Tutorial lesson 5 merges the Confirm step into the stance step; the f02 driver checks that no Confirm follows the stance.
- **F09** (Sanctified Blade, 61): not reproduced in the rules engine. The Medic ability runs only on an explicit `ability` command, then waits for a target choice even with a single legal target; summon, other summons, a Spell cast, a destruction, a stance change and both players' turn starts/ends neither apply Medic nor spend the once-per-turn use; cancelling the target keeps the use; with no adjacent unit it is not offered. The On Summon Heavenly revival is still optional and separate. The owner's observation is most likely the Medic status itself healing at the start of the turn (a status effect, not the ability). Tests: `rules_f09_sanctified_blade_test` (6 tests).
