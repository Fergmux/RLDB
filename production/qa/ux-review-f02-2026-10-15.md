## Defects ranked by severity

### High

**D1. Rotated (Defense) cards rotate the numerals, so "8" reads as the infinity sign.** (F02-03, F02-15)
- Where: every Defense unit. Examples: 04/05 opp card at (557-683, 315-397); 16-19 both sides; `cards/rotated_states.png`. Stat badges, name plate and stance tab all turn 90 degrees. Basic Mage 8/8 shows as "∞ / ∞", and "24/10" and "16" read sideways.
- Why: F02-03 requires "readable stats" on rotated cards. Defense is exactly where DEF matters, and 8 is a very common value. A player scanning the board will read infinite stats. This also fails the art-bible pillar "stats readable at a glance at 1280x800".
- Fix: rotate only the frame and art. Keep the ATK diamond, DEF shield, name and chips upright, anchored to the corners of the 3:2 bounding box (ATK bottom-left, DEF bottom-right). Upright numerals also fix the preview chips and badges on Defense cards. If the whole-card rotation is kept for fidelity, counter-rotate the digits and add a minimum stat size.

**D2. The "ready" teal collides with the Arcane category colour and sits close to selected-blue.** (F02-11, F02-01)
- Where: Tyde (Arcane) on the board is teal-framed in 13-19 and 21 while in Defense or after acting, so it looks "ready" when it is not. Hand and Extra Deck ready rings are `#1fb0a0`-like, the same as the arcane token. `cards/states.png` shows ready+sel with teal and `hl_select` blue only a hue-step apart.
- Why: F02-11 says a unit that cannot act must not advertise an action. Here the frame colour does. The overlap defeats the "no information by colour alone" rule.
- Fix: pick a ready hue that no category or highlight token uses, or make ready shape-first. The star-in-circle glyph at the top-centre is a good cue; make it larger (about 20px) and pair it with a pulsing outer glow that is never a category colour. In parallel, keep Arcane frames at 3px with a teal inner rim only, or shift the arcane token so the two are not identical. Also update the art-bible rule that says gold is the "act here" colour: gold is currently hover, target and response.

### Medium

**D3. Combat preview is hard to read where it matters.** (F02-12)
- Where: 13, 14, 15.
  - Result text is a 12px single line in the top banner, 330-600px away from the cards being compared. At 1280x800 it is ~9.6px, red on dark brown in 15.
  - The "+6 / -6" tag chips on the card are ~8px ("A+6" at (463,510)) and effectively illegible.
  - The preview is signalled only by a thin cyan dotted outline on the changed badge, which is easy to miss. It is not obviously "temporary".
  - The arrow is thin and dashed and passes through the attacker's name plate and the target's ATK diamond. The Even arrow is gold, the same colour as the target rings. Win/lose differ by hue only (the text prefix saves it).
- Fix:
  - Put a compact result chip at the arrowhead: "✓ Win", "✕ Lose", "= Even", 14px+ with an icon. That gives a shape cue for colourblind players.
  - Raise the on-card "+6" tag to ≥11px, or show "8 → 14" in the badge itself.
  - Make the preview badge pulse or use a distinct hatched/outlined style, and say "preview" in the banner line.
  - Start the arrow from the card's top edge rather than its centre, and make Even a neutral grey-white, not gold.
  - Test longest outcome strings (trap caveat plus multiple effects) for banner overflow. Two lines at 46px high have no slack.

**D4. Three states share the same gold.** Hover, valid target and response-ready all use `#ffd84a`-family rings. (F02-01, F02-07, F02-11)
- Where: 07 (all targets), 16/17 (the trap), `cards/states.png` (hover vs target are the same hue, differing only in thickness and fill).
- Why: spec wants these states distinct. In 13-15 the hovered target is hard to distinguish from the other valid targets.
- Fix: keep the target pulse gold. Make hover a lift plus a thin white-gold rim with no fill. Give response-ready a different shape cue (corner chevrons or a "!" badge). Lock a token per state.

**D5. Rotated trap text and rotated names are unreadable.** (F02-03)
- Where: 16/17 own set Mirror Force at (692-822, 706-794); `cards/kinds.png` "trap rotated"; also board names such as "BASIC MAGE ..." on Defense cards are vertical and ~8px.
- Why: the owner cannot tell their own set cards apart without hover. Text is ~8px and vertical.
- Fix: for face-up own traps in landscape zones, use the 3:2 landscape layout (art left, name right, upright text) as the spec describes. Use the name plate, not rules text, on these tiny cards.

**D6. Multi-select tray: selected vs selectable is weak and order badges are tiny.** (F02-10)
- Where: 11. All tray cards have gold rings. Selected ones add only corner brackets and a ~12px number circle at the top-right. Tray 9/11 is a 900x190 panel that overlaps the opponent plate with bleed-through of the dimmed HP bar at (1070, 110).
- Fix: selected = blue ring + a 20px order badge top-left (the "1", "2" large); unselected selectable = no ring, gold hover only. Size the tray to its content and keep it clear of the opponent plate (centre it or dock it under the banner at the board centre).

**D7. The no-target toast has no backing.** (F02-09)
- Where: 08 at (408-830, 55). Plain 13px light text on wood, sitting on the top edge of the opponent trap row. Easy to miss, and it competes with the zone plates.
- Fix: dark pill (`panel_ink` 90% + bronze border), 14-15px, an info icon, 3s fade, stack up to 2. Place it centred under the top strip with 8px clearance from the zones. The log entry is already good.

**D8. Sidebar jumps when the prompt panel grows.** (F02-05, F02-08)
- Where: Card Details top edge moves 195 → 214 → 233 → 273 between 01, 04, 07, 11 and 16 (up to ~80px). Details content, including the scroll position, shifts under the pointer while hovering.
- Fix: reserve a fixed prompt-panel height (or give it its own scroll). Better: since the banner now carries the instruction, shrink the sidebar prompt to the option list only.

**D9. Board card names are ellipsised.** (F02-08, readability)
- Where: every board card shows "BASIC MAGE ..." at 1600 (~9px) and smaller at 1280 (22 shot). Basic Mage (Water) vs (Fire) vs the base are only separated by the pip colour.
- Fix: on board cards show the short name or the first distinct word, or replace the name with the type pip plus a two-line wrap. Details already carry the full name.

### Low

- **D10. Duplicated effect text in details** (F02-08). 06/07 Da Mage: "ON FIELD: Once per turn: you can Apply 1 Pierce to a Unit." is followed by "ABILITIES: Apply Pierce (Once per turn) / Apply 1 Pierce to a unit". `details/unit_busy.png` repeats Blood Price the same way. Fold the ability into its timing block, or show the ability as a button-style chip only.
- **D11. Printed/base mismatch** (F02-08). 13 shows Basic Mage (Fire) "ATK ▲ printed 8" with value 10 but "Modifiers: Base 10/8". Either printed or base is wrong, or the label means something else. Rename ("Printed 10, +0") or fix the data.
- **D12. Banner covers Menu/Settings and series strip**, and the Cancel button is absent in 07 and 09 (present in 04, 11, 13-15). Keep a one-line "Esc to cancel" and the Cancel button in every banner, including mandatory choices (greyed, with the reason). Keep Menu reachable (banner width ≤ board area).
- **D13. Sidebar prompt repeats the banner** with meta-text "The banner above the board explains the choice." Drop that line.
- **D14. Selected-card action stack hangs outside the card** (03, 06, 20), over zone plates and neighbouring cards. Only back-row and hand cases were captured. Capture a front-row unit and a unit with 3-4 actions: below-stacks would cover back-row stats. The spec rule for upward overflow should be checked there. Also in 20 the lifted Spy covers the cost gem and name of Krakeshin.
- **D15. Type pip contrast at card size** (F02-14). `cards/icons.png` shows clear 44px icons. At the ~12-16px pip, the Earth mountain (dark on dark brown), the Lightning bolt (on yellow) and Heavenly (on cream) lose contrast. Give pips a consistent dark disc with a light rim, which also keeps type visible on Lightning and Heavenly art.
- **D16. Stance glyph ambiguity.** The red ✕ in the upright name plate reads as "cancel/disabled", and the amber "−" circle on Defense cards is not self-explanatory. The Physical type icon is also crossed swords, the art-bible attack-stance glyph. Use a distinct stance mark (sword vs shield tab) and make sure Physical is not the same shape.
- **D17. AP slots** (F02-06). Gems are ~14px with a small clock for spent. "AP 5/5" implies a cap of 5 when it is just peak. Consider "AP 5" when above allowance, and slightly larger slots.
- **D18. 3-digit stat in diamond** (F02-15). `cards/field.png` "100/120" fits but touches the badge edge, and the green ▲ buff arrow overlaps the diamond top (10 in 13 and 18). Verify at 1280x800 and in preview (+N tag stacked over the same corner).

## Per-item detail

**F02-01 MEETS.** 02: hover shows details, no stack. 03: click selects and shows the two actions. 16: response lock hides actions. Hidden cards never inspect (report). Selected is clearly different from hover. Remaining issues: D4, D14. The checklist wording "buttons stay on the card" is satisfied only loosely, because the stack sits adjacent to the card.

**F02-02 MEETS.** 03 shows Summon Attack / Summon Defense with no stance menu; 04 goes straight to the zone choice; 05 enters Defense and pays once (AP 2 → 1). Extra Deck shows Arcane Attack / Arcane Defense (10).

**F02-03 PARTIAL.** Upright 85x128 in 128x128 slots (01), 3:2 rotation, and the 1280/1920 proportion checks pass. Fails readability of rotated stats, names and trap text (D1, D5). Upright cards in a square slot show 21px side gutters, which is fine.

**F02-04 MEETS.** 06: "To Defense", "Sacrifice", "Apply Pierce" (13px, no clipping). Details give the full rule and the "Once per turn" chip. Distinguishable on multi-effect cards. Minor: D10.

**F02-05 MEETS.** 21 (and the 01 shot) fits a typical unit without scrolling. Busy cards (`unit_busy.png`) scroll with a visible gold scrollbar and the last status clipped mid-line, which is an acceptable fallback. Log is shorter and still scrollable. See D8.

**F02-06 MEETS.** `details/ap_slots_all.png`: 2/2, 1/2 with one spent, 0/2 both spent, 3/3 for Marked by the Abyss, 3/4 with a 4 peak. 05b shows 5 slots without clipping. See D17.

**F02-07 MEETS.** 04: the banner clearly names the action, and valid zones are highlighted with slot labels. 07: all legal units rimmed. 11: "Selected 2 of 1–3, in the order clicked · cost total 2 / 2", plus Confirm (Enter) and Cancel. The banner stays in the top 46px and covers no board cards. No sidebar field list. Issues: D4, D12, D13, and awkward copy "Da Mage: Apply 1 Pierce to a unit: choose." (better: "Choose 1 unit for Apply Pierce").

**F02-08 PARTIAL.** Strong: identity, category/cost/race/type badges, ATK/DEF panels with delta arrows, stance chips, coloured timing labels (ON SUMMON green, ON FIELD gold, ON DESTRUCTION red, ACTIVATES blue), modifiers list, status explanations, consistent across Unit, Trap, Fusion, Power. Weak: D10, D11. Section headings "RULES/NOTES" are caps but "Modifiers" is not. Fix to one style.

**F02-09 PARTIAL.** Message names the card and effect and is non-blocking, and the log keeps it. Visual presentation: D7.

**F02-10 MEETS.** No Choose button (09, 11). Order kept (2, 1, 0); needs Confirm. Details on hover. See D6.

**F02-11 PARTIAL.** Right information (Defense unit not ready, hand cards unaffordable dim, Battle-phase attackers lit), with a star glyph as a shape cue. Fails on colour collision: D2. Also confirm opponent hidden cards show no ready cue (not visible in 19, which looks correct).

**F02-12 PARTIAL.** Values are correct and match the acceptance example (Water 8 vs Fire 10 shows 14 vs 10, green; Defense 8 vs 8 neutral; the reverse shows DEF 14, red). The hidden-trap caveat is present, and the details pane follows the hovered target. Legibility and the "preview vs committed" cue are weak: D3. Neutral and win/lose are separated by text as well as colour, which is good.

**F02-13 PARTIAL.** The report confirms the events played (phase, turn, card_draw, select, confirm, summon, buff, notice, cancel, attack, destroy). Not shown: that hover does not retrigger, that the opponent's set/hidden actions are silent or generic, volume or mute in Settings, and the asset licence list. Needs a short audio test pass and a note of the assets used.

**F02-14 PARTIAL.** Cohesive set with the same outline weight (`cards/icons.png`), details badge gives icon plus the word "Type: X", and typeless is a plain ring (no fake type). See D15, D16.

**F02-15 MEETS.** Clear diamond ATK; DEF is unchanged. Works at 24 and 120 in the card gallery and in preview (dotted outline). See D18 and D1 (rotated numerals).

## Recommended order of fixes

1. D1 (upright stats on Defense cards). It affects every battle.
2. D2 and D4 (re-assign the highlight palette: ready, hover, target, response, selected, one token each).
3. D3 (combat preview: chip at arrowhead, larger tag, clearer preview cue).
4. D5, D6, D7, D8.
5. Low items. Capture the missing evidence: front-row action stack, 1280x800 with a banner shown, a long outcome string, an audio pass.
