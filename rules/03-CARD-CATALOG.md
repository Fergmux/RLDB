# Card catalogue

## Effect wording guidance — confirmed decision P-24

'Activates an effect' includes effects from Units, Powers, Spells and Traps, including triggered effects such as On Summon and On Destruction. Listeners must not exclude these merely because the effect is not manually activated. All other printed conditions and turn restrictions still apply. Do not treat ordinary recalculation of an already-active continuous modifier as a new activation. This guidance supersedes the narrow prototype definition; see `design/decision-register-answers.md` for the user's answer. Original source CSVs remain unchanged.

All 183 named cards from the supplied database. Stable IDs and original names are retained. Descriptions below include the two confirmed misplaced On Summon effects. Other original wording is preserved; rules and corrections in the accompanying files govern interpretation. Artwork paths are source references, not delivered assets.

Question marks in Type/Race mean none; in ATK/DEF they mean effect-determined. Blank ordinary cost on an extra-deck card is not zero. Ritual and Arcane cost strings retain their distinct cost kinds. Rune keyword references are counters, not buffs.
